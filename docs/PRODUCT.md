# FMB product and business decisions

## Positioning

**Filipino Media Bulletin — Know what matters.** Not another large general-interest newsroom: FMB selects consequential developments, provides an independently written 30–90-second briefing, and offers source links for depth. The customer promise is time saved and understanding gained; it is a product hypothesis to validate, not a superiority claim.

**Core readers:** time-poor working Filipinos, students, and overseas Filipinos. **Core navigation:** Philippines, World, Archive, Sources, Our standards. Categories are secondary metadata, not a maze of top-level pages. Public reading is free and accountless.

## Signature editorial pattern

1. **What happened:** directly evidenced statement, dates, scope and entities, free from unattributed assertions.
2. **Why it matters:** verified practical relevance to Filipinos or a clearly qualified indirect connection; never manufacture impact.
3. **What to watch:** named next decision, deadline, fact still unknown, or update readers should monitor.

The homepage contains a rolling 24-hour window based on original **FMB publication time**; archives persist. Later corrections do not artificially refresh the original publication time. Truly new developments can be covered through new reviewed bulletin records linked to the continuing story in a later phase.

## Tone and visual judgment

- Voice: clear, calm, exact, human, useful; never sensational, condescending or jargon-heavy. Straight reporting separates fact, analysis and uncertainty.
- Headline: specific actor/action and consequence, without loaded words or misleading certainty. Avoid “shocking”, “you won't believe”, and unsupported “all Filipinos affected”.
- Design: distinctive serif editorial headlines with legible system sans body, restrained warm ivory, navy, limited rust accent, intentional white space, text-first hierarchy, actual sourced photography only when rights are established.
- Mobile: avoid overlays, horizontal overflow, dense grids and decorative animations. All controls keyboard-accessible, adequate focus states and reduced motion.
- Distinguish publication from private newsroom software. No generic stock newsroom images or AI-generated eyewitness imagery.

## Business hypothesis and competitor context

The Philippines has established national outlets (GMA News, INQUIRER.net, ABS-CBN News, Philstar, Rappler), specialists (BusinessWorld, VERA Files), and aggregation substitutes (Google News, social platforms). Many already do explainers. FMB should **not** claim it uniquely provides context. It must test whether a curated, stable reading habit is measurably more useful.

**Revenue order:** (1) free reader trust, (2) direct return traffic/newsletter when consent systems exist, (3) clearly labeled sponsorship tests, (4) separately priced institutional briefings if buyers demonstrate demand. Do not copy third-party reporting into a paid product without rights. Disclose sponsors, separate editorial and commercial decision-making, and never sell preferential news coverage as a PR service.

## First 90 days — decision gates

- **Days 1–30:** prove editorial workflow and healthy site. A real test from discovery -> research -> editor approval -> site build -> correction -> retraction, with accurate sourcing and no duplicate publishing.
- **Days 31–60:** test five-minute-reading hypothesis in actual mobile usability tests and measure returning users with consent-compliant measurement. Record comprehension, navigation time and source-click behavior.
- **Days 61–90:** run responsible commercial discovery, e.g. sponsor interviews and institutional briefing pilots. Do not count expressions of interest as revenue.

Metrics: percentage of published bulletins with traceable evidence (target 100%), successful publish operations, correction response time, 30-day return readership, completion of a full briefing, and verified recurring commercial revenue. Quantitative readership and cost forecasts remain unverified until real analytics and invoices exist.

## MVP acceptance gates

- No fictional published stories or unlicensed media.
- Working Philippines / World switch; archive search; stable article URLs.
- Human review and explicit proof-of-work flags before any bulletin enters published data.
- Published/updated times in Asia/Manila, reliable 24-hour expiry.
- Correct source links, visible corrections and retained retraction pages.
- Search metadata added only for actual articles. Noindex until deliberate launch.
- Build/tests pass and responsive layout is reviewed on mobile.

## Commercial red line

Public news cannot be a hidden upsell funnel for SENZ or another PR client. FMB's editorial governance, sponsor disclosure, and conflict register must be independent. Conflicted coverage requires disclosure and independent editorial review.
